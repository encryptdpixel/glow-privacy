Glow Privacy Policy

Effective 5 November 2026 (Glow 1.2.0)

Glow (also called “Glow Home”, package app.glowhome.tv) is a home screen for Android TV and Google TV, made by Pixel Man. This policy explains what information Glow uses and where it goes.

In short

  • Glow has no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking.
  • Everything Glow knows about you stays on your TV. We never receive it, sell it or share it.
  • Glow connects to three public services: one for the weather, one for wallpapers and one to check for new versions. None of them gets your name, account or anything you watch.

Information that stays on your TV

Glow keeps these in its own private storage on the TV. Other apps can't read them, and they are never sent to us or anyone else.

WhatWhy
Guest Wi-Fi name, password and security typeOnly if you enter them. Glow shows them as a QR code so guests can join your network.
Weather cityOnly if you choose one. Otherwise Glow picks a city from the TV's time zone.
The last video or song playedIts title, app and artwork, for up to 3 days, so the Resume banner can take you back to it.
Saved weather and wallpaper informationKept briefly so Glow doesn't download them every time you press Home.
Your app order, hidden apps and screensaver choicesOnly what you set, so the home screen stays the way you arranged it.

Permissions and how Glow uses them

Glow asks you to switch on each of these in Android Settings, and its setup screen explains each one first. Every one is optional: if you leave it off, Glow still works but loses that feature.

PermissionWhat Glow does with it
Notification accessReads what's playing (title, app, artwork, play/pause state) to show the Resume banner and its controls. Glow does not read, store or send the content of your notifications.
Usage accessSees which apps you opened recently to fill the “Recently watched” row. This is read on the TV and never leaves it.
Accessibility service (“Glow Home button”)Detects the remote's Home button, and notices when Google TV's own home screen opens, so Glow can open instead. It does not read what's on screen, see what you type, or collect or share any data.
Photos (“My photos” screensaver)Only if you choose “My photos”, Glow asks to see the pictures stored on the TV or a plugged-in USB drive and shows them in the screensaver and as the wallpaper. They are read on the TV and never uploaded or sent anywhere.
Install apps (“Update now”)Lets Glow install a newer version of itself that it downloaded from Glow's GitHub page. Android asks you to allow this and to confirm each update. Glow checks that the download is Glow, is newer and is signed by its maker, and never installs any other app.
Installed appsLists the apps on your TV so you can open them from the home screen. The list stays on the TV.
TV channels and “Watch next”Reads continue-watching suggestions that other TV apps publish, to show them on the home screen.
InternetUsed only for the three services below.

Services Glow connects to

Like any internet request, these services see your TV's IP address and that the request comes from Glow. Glow sends them nothing else about you.

ServiceWhat Glow sends
MET Norway weather (terms)The coordinates of your weather city, rounded to about 1 km. That's the city centre from Glow's built-in city list, not your TV's actual location. Glow does not use GPS or other location services.
Wikimedia Commons (privacy policy)Requests for lists of featured photos (landscapes, cities, nature or night sky, as you choose) and the wallpaper and screensaver images. Nothing about you or your TV.
GitHub (privacy statement)Once a day, a request for the number and notes of the newest Glow version, so Glow can tell you when an update is out. When you select Update now, the new version's app file is downloaded from Glow's GitHub page. Nothing about you or your TV is sent.

Opening other apps

When you choose Resume for a YouTube video, Glow passes the video's title to the YouTube app on your TV so it can find the video. What YouTube does next is covered by its own privacy policy.

Support link

Glow's setup screen may show a QR code for tipping the developer. Glow itself sends nothing when it's shown; if you scan it, your phone opens that tipping site, which has its own privacy policy.

Children

Glow is a general-audience app and is not directed at children. It collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Deleting your information

To erase everything Glow has stored, go to Settings › Apps › Glow › Clear data on the TV, or uninstall Glow. Since nothing is ever sent to us, there is nothing for us to delete on our side.

Changes to this policy

If Glow ever starts using information differently, we'll update this page and the date at the top before that version of the app is released.
